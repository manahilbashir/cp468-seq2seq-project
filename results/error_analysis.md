## Out-of-vocabulary ceiling (LSTM only)

- 681 / 2890 (23.56%) of test reference tokens are outside the 2,527-token target vocabulary.
- 258 / 270 (95.6%) of references contain at least one such token.
- The LSTM cannot emit these under any decoding strategy, so this is a hard ceiling on its attainable ROUGE. It does not apply to the LLM, which decodes over its own subword vocabulary.
